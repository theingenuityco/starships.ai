## 🌱 Step 1: Describe Your Dream

Your journey begins with a **Mission Prompt** — just describe your idea in natural language.

> “I want to create a climate forecasting tool that runs on open data.”
> 
> 
> “I want to build a research publication about AI and culture.”
> 
> “I want to create a mental health journaling app with a freemium model.”
> 

This is parsed into your initial **Aether Core v0.1**, a structured foundation including:

- 🎯 Goals
- 🧭 Values
- 🚫 Constraints
- 🔍 Focus areas
- 📈 Strategic arcs

## 🔁 Step 2: Expand & Align Your Vision

Next, the **Sibil** collaborates with you to **expand, challenge, and refine** your vision — iteratively.

This is a co-creative dialogue where the system helps you:

- Clarify assumptions and intentions
- Explore possibilities and trade-offs
- Flesh out strategic arcs and focus
- Reframe the idea for more impact

> “Here’s one way your vision could unfold — does this feel aligned?”
> 
> 
> “Would you prefer to optimize for reach, depth, or revenue?”
> 
> “Are multilingual features essential or a roadmap phase?”
> 

You approve each iteration until your **Aether Core** feels like a clear, ambitious, and grounded expression of your intent.

| **Agent** | **Role** |

| **Engineering Agent** | Code, test, deploy |

| **Growth Agent** | Copywriting, outreach, SEO |

| **Ops Agent** | Monitoring, scaling, costs |

You can later upgrade or reassign roles as your mission evolves.
