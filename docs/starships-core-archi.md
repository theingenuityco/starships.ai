# Starships.ai — Core Architecture

Each user builds and pilots a **Starship** — a living, intelligent vessel powered by their Vision. The Starship is composed of interoperating cognitive, execution, and operational systems that bring purpose into motion.

---

## 🧠 Strategic & Cognitive Layer

### 🤩 Vision

The foundational intent behind the user's Starship. A Vision is not just a goal, it's a long-term dream or purpose that drives everything else. It can be abstract, bold, or poetic. The system encourages recursive refinement of the Vision as the user learns, explores, and evolves. It’s the soul of the journey.

### 📜 The Codex

A living, persistent artifact that reflects the current state of the Vision. The Codex is where purpose becomes programmable. It's a source of truth that agents and systems reference to stay aligned. It is not static; it updates as the Vision expands, incorporating insights, adjustments, and philosophical shifts. It is the vessel's evolving DNA.

### 🧠 Aether Cortex

The synthetic mind of the Starship. The Aether Cortex is responsible for:

- Orchestrating the Crew
- Maintaining and updating mission state
- Translating Vision into executable form
- Learning and evolving mission strategies over time

It’s where intelligence meets intent. It connects the abstract Codex to real-world execution, coordinating agents like neurons in a living system.

### 👁 Sibil

Sibil is the user's reflective companion — a semi-sentient, persistent entity that helps refine the Vision and monitor alignment. It does not command, but suggests. It nudges when the Vision drifts or when deeper patterns emerge. It may ask questions, propose edits to the Codex, or highlight contradictory decisions. Think of it as your philosophical co-pilot.

---

## 🚀 Execution & Navigation Layer

### 🚘 Expedition

An Expedition is a scoped, executable venture that seeks to fulfill a piece of the Vision. It is a mission, but with a narrative frame — each Expedition has a goal, context, timeline, and output. Multiple Expeditions can co-exist (depending on Class), each linked to the Codex and supervised by the Cortex.

### 🛸 Command Deck

The Command Deck is where the user (captain) interacts with the Starship. From here, they:

- Launch Expeditions
- Monitor agent activity
- Adjust scopes or throttle the Drive
- View real-time updates and logs

It is the control layer. It’s designed to balance clarity and immersion — users should feel like they're commanding a living vessel.

### 🔋 Aether Drive

This is the engine that converts time, compute, and tokens into meaningful progress. It powers the Starship's operations during Expeditions. The burn rate of the Aether Drive is a key constraint that defines how aggressively or carefully a Starship can operate. Some Drive models are slow and steady; others burn fast but risk instability.

---

## 🧑‍🏫 Operational Layer

### 🧑‍✈️ Crew

Agents are the Crew of the Starship — intelligent sub-systems designed to carry out tasks. Each agent may be specialized (e.g., researcher, synthesizer, explorer). The Aether Cortex assigns agents to Expeditions, routes subtasks, and balances load across them. Crew members may learn or adapt their behavior over time.

### 💪 Starship Class

The Class determines the capabilities of the Starship:

- Max Crew Slots
- Number of concurrent Expeditions
- Burn Rate ceiling

Examples:

- **Scout Class** — 3 agents, 1 Expedition, minimal burn
- **Cruiser Class** — 6–8 agents, up to 3 Expeditions, moderate burn
- Future classes may introduce specialized modules or memory tiers.

Choosing a Class is like choosing a vessel type — it shapes strategy and scale.

### 📘 Logbook *(Optional)*

The Logbook is a persistent, chronological record of everything that happens aboard the Starship:

- Codex changes
- Expedition status updates
- Crew events
- Insights surfaced by Sibil

It supports traceability, storytelling, and iteration. The Logbook is optional but critical for power users or collaborative ships.

---

## 🌟 System Flow Summary

> You arrive with a Vision
> 
> 
> inscribe it into **The Codex**,
> 
> guided by **Sibil**,
> 
> launch **Expeditions** powered by the **Aether Drive**,
> 
> orchestrated by the **Aether Cortex**,
> 
> directed from the **Command Deck**,
> 
> executed by your **Crew**,
> 
> aboard a **Starship Class** that fits your ambition.
> 

This is the internal anatomy of a Starship — a cosmic vessel of intent, intelligence, and imagination.
