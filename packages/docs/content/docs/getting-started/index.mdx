## Your First Steps

### 1. Create Your Account

1. **Sign up** at [starships.ai](https://starships.ai)
2. **Verify your email** (required to access the platform)
3. **Automatic setup**: Upon verification, you'll receive:
   - Your personal starship (workspace)
   - Two specialized AI assistants: **[Gaia](/agents/gaia)** and **[Daedalus](/agents/daedalus)**
   - **$20 credit** to get started

<Callout type="info">
**What's a Starship?** Your starship is your personal AI workspace where all your agents, tools, and conversations live. Think of it as your command center for building with AI. Learn more about the [agent architecture](/core-concepts/agents) and [tool ecosystem](/core-concepts/tools).
</Callout>

### 2. Access Your Starship Dashboard

After email verification, you'll be taken directly to your **conversation interface** at:
`/ship/your-starship-name/conversations`

**Interface Overview:**
- **Left Sidebar**: Navigate between Overview, Conversations, and Marketplace
- **Main Area**: Conversational interface for building with AI
- **Floating Input**: Type `@` to select an AI assistant

### 3. Understand the Chat Interface

Starships.ai uses a **conversational building approach**:

- **Empty state**: "Type @ to choose an agent, then ask your question"
- **Agent selection**: "Ask Gaia..." or "Ask Daedalus..."
- **Building**: Describe what you want in natural language
- **Results**: AI creates agents and tools for you automatically

<Callout type="tip">
**Want to see how it all works?** Check out the [Blueprint architecture](/core-concepts/blueprints) that powers agent workflows behind the scenes.
</Callout>

## Building Your First Agent

### Option 1: Deploy from Marketplace (Fastest)

<div className="my-8">
  <img 
    src="/docs/images/marketplace.png" 
    alt="Marketplace interface showing pre-built agent templates ready for deployment" 
    className="w-full rounded-lg border border-border/20 shadow-lg"
  />
  <p className="text-sm text-muted-foreground text-center mt-2">Browse and deploy pre-built agent templates with one click</p>
</div>

1. **Browse Templates**: Click **[Marketplace](/core-concepts/marketplace)** → Browse categories (Support, Coding, Creative, Analysis)
2. **Deploy**: Select template → Click "Deploy" → Confirm to your starship
3. **Test**: Use `@` to select your new agent and start chatting

**Benefits**: Pre-configured prompts, assigned tools, ready in 1-2 minutes

### Option 2: Build with Gaia (Custom)

<div className="my-8">
  <img 
    src="/docs/images/gaia.png" 
    alt="Gaia conversation interface showing agent creation through natural language" 
    className="w-full rounded-lg border border-border/20 shadow-lg"
  />
  <p className="text-sm text-muted-foreground text-center mt-2">Chat with Gaia to create custom agents tailored to your specific needs</p>
</div>

1. **Start Conversation**: Type `@` → Select **Gaia** → Describe your agent needs
2. **Provide Details**: Answer Gaia's questions about tasks, tone, and requirements  
3. **Deploy & Test**: Gaia creates your agent → Test immediately → Iterate as needed

**Benefits**: Fully customized for your specific use case, 5-10 minutes total
